# Prompt

Animate only the existing logo visible in the input image as a premium cinematic commercial logo reveal.

## SCENE LOCK

Keep the original image completely unchanged and visually identical.

Do not regenerate, redesign, replace, move, crop, distort, alter, or modify the background, architecture, people, objects, lighting, textures, colors, perspective, framing, or camera position.

## LOGO PRESERVATION

Use the exact existing logo from the input image.

Preserve its original typography, letterforms, symbols, proportions, colors, spacing, and design.

Do not redraw, morph, warp, reinterpret, or redesign the logo.

## ANIMATION

At the beginning, the logo is naturally absent or invisible while the entire original scene remains static.

Then reveal the logo progressively, assembling it piece by piece.

Each existing letter or symbol should enter with subtle controlled slide and scale motion, followed by a soft physical settle and a minimal satisfying bounce.

Motion should feel intentional, elegant, premium, and professionally designed, similar to high-end After Effects motion graphics.

## CAMERA

Keep the camera completely locked.

No camera movement, zoom, pan, tilt, parallax, or reframing.

## STYLE

Premium advertising, cinematic, polished motion design, realistic physical movement, smooth timing, restrained and elegant animation.

## FINAL STATE

End with the complete original logo clearly visible in its correct original position and design.

## Negative constraints

No:
- background changes
- object movement
- camera movement
- logo redesign
- logo morphing
- warped letters
- altered typography
- altered colors
- changed perspective
- added objects
- regenerated storefront
- reframing or cropping
