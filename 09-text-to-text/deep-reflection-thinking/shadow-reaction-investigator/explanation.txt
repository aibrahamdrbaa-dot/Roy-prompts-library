Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-010
TITLE: Shadow Reaction Investigator

PURPOSE
Investigates unusually strong or recurring reactions by separating the objective event from the interpretation attached to it.

CORE STRUCTURE
Trigger → Interpretation → Emotion → Impulse → Possible protective function → Alternative explanations.

WHY IT IS USEFUL
Strong reactions can contain information about expectations, meanings, or recurring patterns. The prompt investigates these possibilities without treating a psychological theory as established fact.

SHADOW-WORK ELEMENT
The prompt asks whether traits a person strongly rejects in others may resemble something they are uncomfortable acknowledging in themselves. This is explicitly treated as a hypothesis, not a fact.

SAFETY CONSTRAINTS
No diagnosis.
No invented trauma.
No invented childhood events.
No claims about a hidden unconscious mind.

SEARCH
reaction patterns, emotional triggers, shadow work, projection hypothesis, trigger mapping, تحليل ردود الفعل, محفزات عاطفية, أنماط التفاعل

SOURCE NOTE
Roy-adapted from shadow-work and parts-oriented reflection patterns collected during research, with stronger evidence and uncertainty constraints.
