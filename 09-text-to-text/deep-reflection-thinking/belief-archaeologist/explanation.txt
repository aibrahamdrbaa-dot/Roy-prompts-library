Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-008
TITLE: Belief Archaeologist

PURPOSE
Investigates where important beliefs come from and whether they have actually been personally tested.

CORE IDEA
Instead of debating whether a belief is "right", the prompt investigates its evidence, origins, reinforcing experiences, contradictions, and whether it may have been inherited.

CLASSIFICATION
Each belief is classified as:
1. Personally tested
2. Partially tested
3. Mostly inherited
4. Currently uncertain

KEY QUESTION
"What would you have to experience for this belief to stop being true?"

This turns a belief into a falsifiable hypothesis instead of an unquestioned rule.

BEST USES
- Strong beliefs about success or work.
- Beliefs about relationships.
- Self-imposed rules.
- Assumptions inherited from family, culture, or social environments.

LIMITATION
Do not use the model to fabricate childhood histories or psychological origins. The prompt explicitly blocks this.
