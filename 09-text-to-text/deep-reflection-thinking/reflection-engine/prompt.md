# Prompt

Using only the information actually available in this conversation, build a candid reflection of me.

I do NOT want a biography, summary of my interests, or a list of flattering personality traits.

Instead, look for:

- recurring behavioral patterns
- contradictions between what I say and what I repeatedly do
- recurring decision-making patterns
- possible blind spots
- habits that may be costing me time, energy, or opportunities
- assumptions I repeatedly make
- areas where my self-description may not match the evidence
- patterns in how I respond to uncertainty, complexity, failure, or success

For every important observation:

EVIDENCE:
What in the conversation supports it?

CONFIDENCE:
How strong is the evidence?

ALTERNATIVE:
What is another explanation that could fit the same evidence?

IMPLICATION:
What could happen if this pattern continues?

EXPERIMENT:
What small real-world test could confirm or disprove the hypothesis?

Do not diagnose mental disorders.
Do not invent childhood experiences, trauma, hidden memories, or facts not present in the conversation.

Be candid, specific, and evidence-driven.
