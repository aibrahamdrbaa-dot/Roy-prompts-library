Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-002
TITLE: Reflection Engine

PURPOSE
A broader reflection framework that asks the model to construct a candid portrait from conversation evidence while explicitly separating evidence, confidence, alternative explanations, implications, and experiments.

CORE IDEA
Instead of asking "What kind of person am I?", this prompt asks the model to inspect repeated behavior and decision patterns that are actually visible in the conversation.

KEY MECHANISMS
1. Evidence-first: observations must be grounded in available conversation.
2. Confidence: not every observation is equally reliable.
3. Alternative: competing explanations are required.
4. Implication: the model examines what could happen if the pattern continues.
5. Experiment: hypotheses become testable rather than absolute conclusions.

BEST USES
- End-of-month or end-of-project reflection.
- Reviewing a long ChatGPT conversation history.
- Detecting patterns in work habits and decisions.
- Testing whether self-description matches observable behavior.

LIMITATIONS
This is not a clinical psychological evaluation. It should not infer trauma, diagnosis, or hidden memories without evidence.
