Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-006
TITLE: Problem Defrag

PURPOSE
Use this when a problem feels stuck after several attempts. The prompt deliberately delays solution generation and first interrogates the problem definition.

CORE QUESTIONS
- What do I think the problem is?
- What could actually be causing it?
- What have I already tried?
- What failed?
- Which constraints are real?
- Which constraints may be assumptions?
- What evidence is missing?
- Is the problem framed correctly?

FINAL OUTPUT
After enough questioning, the model:
A. reframes the problem
B. proposes a hidden variable
C. suggests 3 unusual approaches
D. identifies which assumption each approach breaks

WHY IT IS DIFFERENT
Many AI prompts optimize for answering quickly. This one optimizes for discovering whether you are solving the right problem in the first place.

BEST USES
- Stubborn business problems.
- Productivity bottlenecks.
- Project blockers.
- Decisions that keep looping.
- Problems where every obvious solution has already failed.
