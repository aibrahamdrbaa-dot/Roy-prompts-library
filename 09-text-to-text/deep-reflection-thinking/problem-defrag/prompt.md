# Prompt

I have a persistent problem that I have already tried to solve several times.

Do not give me solutions yet.

First, interrogate the problem.

Ask me one question at a time and continue until you understand:
- what I believe the problem is
- what may actually be causing it
- what I already tried
- what failed and why
- what constraints are real
- which constraints I may have invented
- what I am assuming without evidence
- what part of the problem I may be framing incorrectly

Once you have enough information, stop asking questions and do this:

A. Define the problem in a completely different way.
B. Identify the hidden variable.
C. Give me 3 approaches I probably would not have considered.
D. Explain which assumption each approach breaks.

Do not rush to solution mode.
