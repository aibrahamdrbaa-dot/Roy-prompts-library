Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-007
TITLE: The Role I Hide Behind

PURPOSE
Investigates recurring identities or roles a person relies on in different situations instead of trying to label their entire personality.

CORE IDEA
People often describe themselves through roles such as achiever, expert, helper, rational thinker, perfectionist, or problem-solver. This prompt investigates when a role appears, what it enables, and what it may protect.

WHY IT IS INTERESTING
The prompt does not assume the role is negative. A role can be highly useful while also creating blind spots when it becomes the default response.

PROCESS
Role → Trigger → Function → Protection → Evidence → Alternative explanation → Cost/benefit → Experiment.

BEST USES
- Repeated behavior patterns.
- Leadership or work identity.
- Perfectionism-style patterns.
- Exploring why a person feels uncomfortable when they cannot "be" a certain kind of person.

SEARCH

LIMITATION
This is reflective hypothesis generation, not diagnosis or a clinical personality assessment.
