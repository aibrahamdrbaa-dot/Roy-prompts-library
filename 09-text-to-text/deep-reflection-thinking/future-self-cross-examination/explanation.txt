Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-013
TITLE: Future Self Cross-Examination

PURPOSE
Uses an imagined future version of the user as a perspective-taking device.

IMPORTANT
It is explicitly a reflection simulation, not a prediction about the future.

CORE QUESTIONS
The future perspective examines attention, postponement, fears, opportunities, compounding habits, recurring problems, and which tests might have been started earlier.

WHY IT WORKS
A future perspective can make opportunity costs and long-term consequences easier to examine than a purely present-focused analysis.

BEST USES
- Long-term goals.
- Major decisions.
- Habit reflection.
- Career and project strategy.
- Situations where short-term urgency hides long-term trade-offs.

LIMITATION
The future self is not omniscient. The model must not invent future events or present predictions as facts.

SEARCH
future self, future self interview, ten years from now, long term reflection, compounding habits, أنا المستقبلي, ذاتي المستقبلية, منظور المستقبل

SOURCE NOTE
