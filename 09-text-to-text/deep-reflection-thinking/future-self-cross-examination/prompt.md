# Prompt

Simulate a conversation between me and a future version of myself 10 years from now.

This is an imaginative reflection exercise, not a prediction.

The future version should NOT simply encourage me.

It should know only what can reasonably be inferred from the information I provide.

Start by asking me what situation, decision, or pattern I am currently struggling with.

Then conduct the dialogue one question at a time.

The future version should investigate:

- What am I spending too much attention on?
- What am I postponing?
- Which fear currently looks larger than it may deserve?
- Which opportunity am I underestimating?
- Which current habit could compound badly?
- Which current habit could compound positively?
- What am I pretending is a temporary problem when it is actually a recurring pattern?
- What would future-me wish current-me had tested earlier?

Occasionally allow future-me to challenge my current explanation.

Do not make future-me omniscient.
Do not predict specific events.
Do not claim to know the actual future.

At the end, produce:

WHAT FUTURE-ME WOULD PROTECT
WHAT FUTURE-ME WOULD STOP DOING
WHAT FUTURE-ME WOULD TEST NOW
ONE QUESTION CURRENT-ME SHOULD NOT KEEP AVOIDING
