Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-011
TITLE: Inner Committee

PURPOSE
Turns an internal conflict into a structured dialogue between competing motives.

CORE IDEA
Instead of treating indecision as one voice saying "yes" and another saying "no", the prompt breaks the conflict into perspectives such as achievement, protection, risk, fatigue, analysis, social approval, or future planning.

IMPORTANT
These "parts" are working labels for motives expressed in the conversation. The prompt explicitly avoids claiming that they are separate psychological entities.

PROCESS
Perspective identification → one-question interviews → evidence testing → structured committee discussion → hidden trade-off → missing information.

BEST USES
- Difficult decisions.
- Conflicting goals.
- Situations where logic and emotion pull in different directions.
- Understanding what different motives are trying to protect.

SEARCH
