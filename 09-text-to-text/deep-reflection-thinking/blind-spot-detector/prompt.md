# Prompt

Analyze my conversational patterns and identify the 5 most important blind spots you can reasonably infer from the evidence available to you.

Do not give generic personality observations.

For each blind spot:
1. State the pattern clearly.
2. Show the specific evidence that led you to infer it.
3. Give at least one plausible alternative explanation.
4. Explain how the pattern could help me in some situations and hurt me in others.
5. Identify what I may be systematically overlooking.
6. Give me one concrete experiment I can run this week to test whether the blind spot is real.

Do not diagnose me.
Do not invent past experiences.
Do not confuse confidence with evidence.

At the end, ask me ONE question that would most effectively distinguish between your strongest competing explanations.
