Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-001
TITLE: Blind Spot Detector

PURPOSE
Use this as an interactive text-to-text analysis when you want the model to identify recurring blind spots from actual conversational evidence instead of giving generic personality descriptions.

WHAT MAKES IT STRONG
- Evidence requirement: every inference must point back to observable conversation.
- Alternative explanation: the model must consider another interpretation instead of treating its first hypothesis as fact.
- Cost/benefit view: the same pattern is examined for both advantages and disadvantages.
- Systematic omission: the prompt explicitly asks what the user may be overlooking.
- Behavioral experiment: it turns a psychological-style hypothesis into something testable in real life.
- Final discriminating question: the model must ask one question designed to separate competing explanations.

BEST USES
- After a long series of ChatGPT conversations.
- When you suspect a recurring habit or decision pattern.
- When you want evidence-based self-reflection rather than compliments.
- When you want hypotheses that can be tested rather than labels.

LIMITATIONS
This is a reflection exercise, not a clinical psychological assessment. The model only sees the evidence available in the conversation and may miss important context.

SEARCH

STRUCTURE
Pattern → Evidence → Alternative → Impact → Overlooked factor → Experiment → Discriminating question.
