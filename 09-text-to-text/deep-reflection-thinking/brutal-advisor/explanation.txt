Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-004
TITLE: Brutal Advisor

PURPOSE
A candid critique prompt for plans, strategies, projects, or decisions. It is designed to attack weak reasoning without turning the interaction into personal abuse.

CORE MECHANIC
The model searches for underestimation, avoidance, weak assumptions, rationalization, and failure modes.

IMPORTANT DESIGN CHOICE
The prompt explicitly says:
- distinguish evidence from inference
- give the strongest counterargument
- state what evidence would prove the criticism wrong

This reduces the common failure mode where "brutal" simply becomes confident negativity.

BEST USES
- Strategy reviews.
- Project decisions.
- Business plans.
- Personal productivity systems.
- Testing whether a plan is actually moving forward.

SEARCH
brutal advisor, candid advisor, strategic critique, rationalization, activity vs progress, مستشار صريح, نقد استراتيجي

NOTE
Use "intellectually ruthless, not emotionally aggressive" to keep the output useful rather than insulting.

SOURCE NOTE
Roy-adapted and structured from candid-advisor prompt patterns collected during research.
