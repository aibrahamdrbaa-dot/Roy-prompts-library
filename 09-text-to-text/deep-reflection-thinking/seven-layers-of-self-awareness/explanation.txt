Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-005
TITLE: Seven Layers of Self-Awareness

PURPOSE
A seven-step interactive interview where each question is deeper than the previous one.

PROGRESSION
The prompt starts with observable behavior and gradually explores:
behavior → values → motivations → recurring choices → fears/avoidance → contradictions → self-protective or optimization patterns.

AFTER EACH ANSWER
The model reflects briefly, proposes one possible pattern, challenges part of the answer, then asks the next question.

WHY IT WORKS AS AN EXPERIMENT
It prevents the model from writing a huge "personality report" based on one message. The evidence is generated progressively through dialogue.

BEST USES
- Self-discovery.
- Clarifying motivations.
- Exploring a recurring decision.
- Understanding why you repeatedly choose similar options.

SEARCH
seven layers, self awareness, self discovery, progressive interview, deep questions, سبع طبقات, اكتشاف الذات, وعي ذاتي

LIMITATION
This is a reflective conversation exercise, not a clinical assessment.

SOURCE NOTE
Roy-adapted and structured from the progressive seven-question prompt pattern collected during research.
