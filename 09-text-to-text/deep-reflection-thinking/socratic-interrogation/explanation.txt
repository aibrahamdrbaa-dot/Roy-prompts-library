Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-003
TITLE: Socratic Interrogation

PURPOSE
This turns ChatGPT into an interactive Socratic interviewer. It is useful when you want to discover assumptions rather than receive an immediate answer.

CORE MECHANIC
The model asks only one question at a time. Every next question must depend on your previous response.

WHAT IT TARGETS
- Vague definitions.
- Hidden assumptions.
- Evidence behind beliefs.
- Contradictions.
- Conditions that would change your mind.
- The difference between facts and interpretations.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
Most advice prompts produce an answer immediately. This one deliberately withholds the solution and uses the conversation itself as the investigation.

BEST USES
- Decisions.
- Strong personal beliefs.
- Business ideas.
- Ethical questions.
- Difficult choices.
- Problems where you suspect your framing is incomplete.

GOOD INPUT FORMAT
State one belief, decision, or problem clearly, then let the dialogue proceed.

SEARCH
