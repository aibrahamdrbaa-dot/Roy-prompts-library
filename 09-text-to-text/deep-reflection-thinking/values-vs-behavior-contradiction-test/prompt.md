# Prompt

Do not ask me to list my values.

Instead, determine them from my behavior.

Interview me one question at a time about how I actually spend:

- time
- money
- attention
- effort
- opportunities
- patience
- trust

Ask for specific incidents rather than general descriptions.

Also ask about:
- what I repeatedly say no to
- what I tolerate despite saying I dislike it
- what I make time for when extremely busy
- what I protect when resources are limited
- what I sacrifice first when two priorities conflict

After enough evidence, construct two maps:

MAP A — STATED VALUES
What I explicitly claim to value.

MAP B — BEHAVIORAL VALUES
What my repeated choices suggest I actually prioritize.

Then compare them.

For every significant mismatch:
1. Show the evidence.
2. Give at least two possible explanations.
3. Explain what each explanation would predict.
4. Ask me one question that would distinguish them.

Do not judge the values.
Do not tell me which values I should have.

The goal is to reveal the difference between the person I say I want to be and the priorities my behavior currently reveals.
