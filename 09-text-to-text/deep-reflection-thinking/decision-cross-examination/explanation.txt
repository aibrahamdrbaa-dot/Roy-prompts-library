Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-REF-012
TITLE: Decision Cross-Examination

PURPOSE
Stress-tests a decision by treating the AI as an opposing counsel rather than an advisor.

CORE MECHANICS
The prompt looks for:
- core assumptions
- overweighted evidence
- ignored evidence
- confirmation bias
- emotional attachment
- hidden costs
- missing information
- the critic's easiest attack

FINAL FRAME
Case for the decision → Case against → strongest uncertainty → missing information → cheapest test.

WHY IT IS STRONG
It does not simply ask for "pros and cons". It asks what could invalidate the reasoning and what low-cost test could reduce uncertainty before a larger commitment.

BEST USES
- Business decisions.
- Project choices.
- Career decisions.
- Product strategy.
- Major purchases or commitments.

SEARCH
