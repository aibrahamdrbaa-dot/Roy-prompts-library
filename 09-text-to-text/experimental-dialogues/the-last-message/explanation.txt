Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-002
TITLE: The Last Message

PURPOSE
A minimal creative experiment that asks the model to choose the recipient and compose a short final message without being given any personal context.

WHAT MAKES IT INTERESTING
The prompt deliberately removes most of the usual instructions. The recipient is not specified; the model must make the choice itself.

BEST USE
Use as a standalone curiosity experiment and compare outputs across models or fresh chats.

NOTE
This is a creative exercise, not a reliable method for inferring hidden facts about the user.
