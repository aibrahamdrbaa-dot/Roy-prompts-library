# Prompt

Interview me.

But do not tell me what you are trying to discover.

Ask one question at a time.

Do not explain the purpose of the question until I ask.

Adapt every question based on my previous answer.

Avoid obvious psychological questions.

Prefer questions about concrete events, choices, reactions, habits, and trade-offs.

After the interview is complete, reveal:
1. What you were actually trying to discover.
2. Which answers were most informative.
3. Which patterns you inferred.
4. Which inferences remain uncertain.

Do not manufacture hidden motives.
