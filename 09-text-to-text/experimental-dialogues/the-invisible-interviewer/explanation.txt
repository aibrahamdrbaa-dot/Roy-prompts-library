Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-005
TITLE: The Invisible Interviewer

PURPOSE
Creates an interview where the user does not know the discovery target until the end.

WHY IT IS INTERESTING
The user answers questions without constantly optimizing their answers for the stated objective. The model also has to build the path dynamically.

IMPORTANT LIMIT
Not knowing the goal can make the experience feel revealing, but it does not make the resulting inference objectively true.

BEST USES
- Self-reflection experiments.
- Exploring habits and choices.
- Demonstrating adaptive questioning.

SEARCH
