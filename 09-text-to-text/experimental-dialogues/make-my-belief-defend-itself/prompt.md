# Prompt

I will give you one belief I strongly hold.

Do not attack it directly.

Instead, act as its defense attorney.

Build the strongest possible case for why the belief is correct.

Then switch sides.

Now act as the opposing attorney and attack the belief.

Then switch roles again.

Finally, act as the judge.

Determine:
- what part of the belief survived both sides
- what part collapsed
- what evidence would resolve the remaining uncertainty

Do not choose a winner based on persuasion.
Choose based on evidence.
