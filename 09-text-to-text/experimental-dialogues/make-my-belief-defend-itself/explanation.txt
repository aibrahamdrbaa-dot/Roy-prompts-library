Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-006
TITLE: Make My Belief Defend Itself

PURPOSE
Stress-tests a strongly held belief through sequential role changes: defense attorney → opposing counsel → judge.

WHY IT IS STRONG
Starting with the strongest defense prevents the critique from attacking a weak caricature. The final stage focuses on evidence rather than rhetorical persuasion.

BEST USES
- Strong personal beliefs.
- Business assumptions.
- Strategy decisions.
- Intellectual disagreements.

KEY CONCEPT
Steelman first, attack second, adjudicate last.

SEARCH
belief defense, devil's advocate, opposing counsel, belief stress test, steelman, اختبار القناعة, محامي الخصم
