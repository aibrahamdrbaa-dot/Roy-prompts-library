# Prompt

I am going to give you a problem.

Do not solve it.

Instead, determine the most important question I have failed to ask.

You may ask me questions first.

Ask only one question at a time.

Your goal is to discover the missing question that, if answered, could radically change how the problem should be framed.

Do not reveal your hypothesis until you are sufficiently confident.

At the end:
- state the missing question
- explain why it matters
- show what assumptions my original framing contained
- describe how the problem changes once the missing question is included
