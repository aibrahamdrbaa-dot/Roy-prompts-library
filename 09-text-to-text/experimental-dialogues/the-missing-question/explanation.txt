Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-004
TITLE: The Missing Question

PURPOSE
Finds a question that is absent from the user's framing but could materially change the problem.

CORE IDEA
The model is prohibited from solving the stated problem until it has investigated whether the framing itself is incomplete.

WHY IT IS STRONG
Many difficult problems are difficult partly because the wrong question is being optimized. This experiment attacks the framing layer first.

BEST USES
- Business strategy.
- Research questions.
- Personal decisions.
- Problems that produce repetitive failed solutions.

SEARCH
missing question, question behind the question, problem framing, reframing, السؤال المفقود, إعادة تأطير المشكلة
