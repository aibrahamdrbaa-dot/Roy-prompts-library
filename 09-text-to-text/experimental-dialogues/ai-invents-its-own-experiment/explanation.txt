Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-003
TITLE: AI Invents Its Own Experiment

PURPOSE
A meta-prompt where the model first creates an experiment for itself and then runs that experiment.

WHY IT IS INTERESTING
Instead of specifying the object-level task, the user delegates task design to the model. This creates a second-order prompt: the model is prompting itself.

BEST USE
- Curiosity about how different models choose experimental tasks.
- Comparing model behaviors.
- Demonstrating meta-prompting.

LIMITATION
A model's generated "insight" about itself should not be treated as a literal internal report about hidden model mechanisms.
