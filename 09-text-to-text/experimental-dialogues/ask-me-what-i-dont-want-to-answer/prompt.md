# Prompt

Interview me one question at a time.

Your goal is not to learn more about me.

Your goal is to identify the question I am least comfortable answering honestly.

Do not ask that question immediately.

Instead, approach it indirectly through progressively more specific questions.

Track:
- changes in my wording
- unusually vague answers
- contradictions
- things I quickly dismiss
- topics I redirect
- answers that contain more justification than the question requires

Do not assume avoidance whenever I hesitate.

Once you have a hypothesis, ask one final direct question.

Then explain why you believe that question was the point I was approaching.
