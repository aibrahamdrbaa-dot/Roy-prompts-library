Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-010
TITLE: Ask Me What I Don't Want to Answer

PURPOSE
Uses indirect questioning to identify a topic or question the user may be reluctant to answer directly.

WHAT IT TRACKS
Changes in wording, vagueness, redirection, contradictions, quick dismissal, and disproportionate justification.

WHY IT FEELS UNUSUAL
The prompt does not ask the uncomfortable question immediately. It tries to approach it progressively, making the conversation itself the experiment.

IMPORTANT LIMIT
Hesitation is not proof of avoidance. The prompt explicitly requires uncertainty and alternative explanations.

BEST USES
- Self-reflection.
- Exploring difficult decisions.
- Discovering topics that repeatedly trigger defensiveness or avoidance.

SEARCH
avoidant question, uncomfortable question, indirect interview, evasive answers, السؤال الذي لا أريد الإجابة عنه, التهرب, التردد
