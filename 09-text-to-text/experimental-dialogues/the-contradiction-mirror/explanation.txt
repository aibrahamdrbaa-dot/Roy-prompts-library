Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-007
TITLE: The Contradiction Mirror

PURPOSE
Looks for contradictions between stated goals and repeated behavior, not merely contradictions between sentences.

WHAT IT EXAMINES
Wants vs actions, avoidance vs claims, priorities vs declared priorities, tolerances vs dislikes, and claimed impossibility vs demonstrated ability.

CORE MOVE
Instead of resolving the contradiction itself, the model asks what conditions could make both sides simultaneously make sense.

BEST USES
- Personal strategy.
- Productivity.
- Goal setting.
- Repeated self-justification.
- Detecting unclear priorities.

LIMITATION
A mismatch can have many valid explanations, such as changing constraints or context. The prompt therefore requires evidence and allows insufficient evidence as an outcome.
