# Prompt

Analyze what I tell you for contradictions.

Do not look only for contradictions between sentences.

Look for contradictions between:
- what I say I want
- what I spend time doing
- what I avoid
- what I repeatedly prioritize
- what I tolerate
- what I claim is impossible
- what I repeatedly make possible

When you find a contradiction:

DO NOT resolve it for me.

Present both sides as strongly as possible.

Then ask me one question:

"What would have to be true for both of these things to make sense at the same time?"

Continue until we either resolve the contradiction or prove that the evidence is insufficient.
