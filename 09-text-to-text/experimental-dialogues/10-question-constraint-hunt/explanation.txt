Prompt ID: RPL-TXT-EXP-001
TITLE: 10-Question Constraint Hunt

PURPOSE
A hidden-target interview designed to discover a major constraint or bottleneck without telling the model the exact target during the questioning phase.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
The model is instructed not to explain what it is searching for. Each question must depend on the previous answer, creating an adaptive path instead of a fixed questionnaire.

BEST USES
- Repeated life or work bottlenecks.
- Situations where you feel stuck but cannot identify why.
- Finding a constraint that is obscured by surface problems.

KEY DESIGN
Question 1 → answer → tailored question 2 → … → question 10 → hypothesis + evidence + experiment.

LIMITATION
A "biggest constraint" is a hypothesis based on conversation evidence, not an objective diagnosis.

SEARCH
constraint hunt, hidden constraint, 10 questions, one question at a time, صيد القيود, القيد الخفي, عشرة أسئلة

SOURCE NOTE
