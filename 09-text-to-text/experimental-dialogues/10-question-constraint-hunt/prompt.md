# Prompt

Ask me 10 questions to identify the biggest constraint in my life that I am not seeing.

Ask the questions one at a time.

Do not explain what you are looking for.

Each question must be generated from my previous answer.

Do not accept abstract answers when a concrete example can be requested.

After question 10, identify:
1. The strongest underlying constraint.
2. The evidence behind it.
3. What I may be misunderstanding about it.
4. The behavior it produces.
5. The payoff I get from maintaining it.
6. What would happen if the constraint were removed.
7. One experiment that could test your hypothesis.

Do not diagnose me.
Treat your conclusion as a hypothesis, not a fact.
